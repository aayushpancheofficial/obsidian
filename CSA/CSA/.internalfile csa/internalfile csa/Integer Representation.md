# Integer Representation

## Meaning

Integer representation is the method used to store integer numbers in binary form.

## Types

- Unsigned representation
- Signed magnitude representation
- 1's complement representation
- 2's complement representation

## Range

For n-bit unsigned number:

```text
0 to 2^n - 1
```

For n-bit 2's complement number:

```text
-2^(n-1) to +2^(n-1)-1
```

## Exam Answer

Integers are represented in binary using unsigned and signed forms. Signed integers may use signed magnitude, 1's complement, or 2's complement. 2's complement is most commonly used because addition and subtraction are simple.
