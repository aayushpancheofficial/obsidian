# Signed Numbers

## Meaning

Signed numbers represent both positive and negative numbers in binary.

## Methods

| Method | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Signed magnitude | MSB is sign bit |
| 1's complement | Negative number formed by inverting bits |
| 2's complement | Negative number formed by 1's complement + 1 |

## Sign Bit

```text
0 = positive
1 = negative
```

## Exam Answer

Signed numbers use the most significant bit as sign bit. 2's complement is widely used because arithmetic operations are easier and there is only one representation of zero.
