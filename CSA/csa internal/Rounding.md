# Rounding

## Meaning

Rounding is the process of adjusting a number to fit into available precision.

## Need

Floating point operations may produce more bits than can be stored. Rounding gives the nearest representable value.

## Methods

- Round toward zero
- Round to nearest
- Round up
- Round down

## Exam Answer

Rounding is used in floating point arithmetic when the result has more digits than the available mantissa field. It helps control precision and error.
