# Integer Division

## Meaning

Integer division performs binary division and gives quotient and remainder.

## Types

- Restoring division
- Non-restoring division

## Restoring Division

If A becomes negative after subtraction, restore A by adding divisor back.

## Non-Restoring Division

If A becomes negative, do not restore immediately. Next step decides add or subtract.

## Exam Answer

Integer division is performed using shift and add/subtract operations. Restoring division restores negative remainder immediately, while non-restoring division avoids immediate restoration and is faster.
