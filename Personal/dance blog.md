# How I Accidentally Fell in Love with Dance

I was never a trained dancer.

I never joined a dance class.  
I never won dance competitions.  
Honestly, I still forget steps while performing.

But somehow...

Dance has always been a part of my life.

## It all started with dance shows.

When I was a kid, I mostly watched cartoons.

There was one show I never missed—**Baalveer**. It used to air Monday to Saturday at 8 PM. But on weekends there wasn't much to watch.

That's when I randomly started watching dance reality shows.

And...

I loved them.

Every Saturday and Sunday I would wait for the next episode. Every performance felt different. New music, new concepts, new emotions.

Whenever one show ended, another one started.

Sometimes it was **Dance Plus**.  
Sometimes **Super Dancer**.  
Sometimes some other dance show on another channel.

I didn't care about the channel.

If it had dance, I watched it.

Slowly, I started enjoying music too. Every act taught me something without anyone actually teaching me.

---

## The secret nobody knew

I was a very shy kid.

I could never dance in front of people.

My parents were usually out for work, so I spent a lot of time alone at home.

That's where my dance floor was.

I would copy random moves.

Sometimes I tried stunts.

Sometimes I just moved with the music.

Sometimes I made up my own steps.

I didn't know any dance style.

I didn't even know if what I was doing could be called dancing.

I just knew...

Whenever I watched someone dance, my heart wanted to dance too.

---

## School days

In every school function, I watched other students perform.

Deep inside, I wanted to be on that stage too.

But there was one problem.

I was extremely shy.

And I thought...

_"Dance to aata hi nahi."_

So I never participated.

Not once.

The only time I danced was in one group performance where I quietly stood in the last row.

That was my entire school dance career.

---

## Then life changed.

Board exams came.

After that, JEE preparation started.

For almost four years, studies became my only priority.

I completely stopped watching dance shows.

I stopped dancing.

I thought this was necessary because education was my way to build a better future.

Dance quietly disappeared from my life.

---

## But it never really left.

During my JEE preparation, I lived alone in a room.

Whenever I felt stressed...

I played music.

Sometimes I watched dance videos.

Sometimes I danced alone.

Not because I was good.

Just because it made me feel better.

I still remember recording this random clip late at night after studying for hours.

I was tired.

I wanted a break.

So I played a song and started creating random steps.

I had no choreography.

No practice.

Just music.

## Then came college.

A few days after joining college, there was a talent event between sections.

Almost everyone was participating.

Somewhere inside me...

I wanted to dance too.

I walked up to one of the hosts.

**Me:** "Dance kar sakte hain kya?"

**Host:** "Haan. Bas apna naam bhej do."

I thought...

"Hostel jaake prepare karta hoon."

But I never practiced.

I never even submitted my name.

Or at least...

That's what I thought.

---

## The moment I'll never forget.

Event day.

Students were performing one after another.

I was sitting quietly.

Then suddenly...

The host called my name.

"Aayush..."

For a second, my heartbeat stopped.

My entire class started shouting.

I had two options.

Sit there...

Or walk onto the stage.

So I stood up.

And walked.

Believe me...

I had never performed in front of so many people.

Around 150 students were watching.

I didn't know proper dance.

I hadn't practiced.

My confidence was almost zero.

Inside my head it was complete chaos.

But once the music started...

I just danced.

However I could.

Some steps were wrong.

Some were random.

Some probably made no sense.

But I finished.

When I came back, everyone was cheering.

People appreciated my performance.

Among all the boys, I was the only one who had performed solo.

I still have that photo.

I'm standing in the last row after the event.

![[1st d.jpeg]]

But honestly...

I wasn't satisfied.

I knew I could do better.

---

## That day changed something.

I made one promise to myself.

The thing I loved as a kid...

I wouldn't let it disappear again.

I started dancing whenever I found time.

Not every day.

Not professionally.

Just consistently.

Dance kept me active.

It made my body flexible.

More importantly...

It made me happy.

---

## The second performance

I participated in another college event.

This time I tried adding emotions instead of just steps.

Funny thing?

I still forgot the choreography.

While dancing I was literally thinking...

_"Yaar... ye song khatam kyun nahi ho raha?"_

As soon as the music stopped...

I quietly went back to my seat.

Every performance had a different emotion attached to it.

## The performance I'll always remember

A few months later came another event.

This time I chose **Kaise Hua**.

I wanted to perform a contemporary-style dance.

The timing couldn't have been worse.

The previous night I had been volunteering for **Navonmesh** and barely slept.

I was exhausted.

I didn't even want to perform.

I had actually gone there just to watch others dance.

A friend told me,

> "Solo performance mein itni preparation kaun karta hai? Jo feel ho, woh kar."

That sentence stayed with me.

When my turn came...

I stopped thinking.

I simply felt the song.

No complicated choreography.

No perfect technique.

Just emotions.

Halfway through the performance...

The audience started singing along.

Their energy gave me even more confidence.

That feeling...

I still can't describe it.

It wasn't my best dance.

But it was one of my favorite moments.

## What dance means to me today


Today, dance means something completely different to me than it did when I was a kid. Back then, I simply enjoyed watching people perform. Now, dance has become a part of who I am. Sometimes, when the music starts, it feels like I'm entering a different world. For a few minutes, nothing else matters. It's just me, the music, and the emotions I'm carrying at that moment.

I don't dance to prove that I'm the best or because I know every step perfectly. I dance because it's the easiest way for me to express feelings that words can't always explain. If I'm happy, I dance. If I'm sad, I dance. If I'm overthinking, I dance. Every emotion has its own rhythm, and every song tells a different story. I simply become a part of that story.

For me, music and dance can never be separated. Music creates the emotion, and dance gives that emotion a body. That's why I believe life would feel incomplete without them. I may not be a great singer, but whenever a song touches my heart, I let my body tell the story. Maybe that's what dance really is—not perfect moves, but honest feelings.

---

## This is just the beginning.

I'm not a great dancer, and I still have a lot to learn.

I don't dance every day. In fact, I dance very rarely—only when I genuinely feel like it. I don't force it because, for me, dance is something that comes from the heart.

This blog is a promise to myself. Whenever life gets busy and I start forgetting this part of me, I'll come back here and remember why I fell in love with dance in the first place.

Whenever I learn a new move, perform on a stage, dance to a new song, or simply enjoy dancing alone in my room, I'll share it here.

Dance and music make life a little more colorful. Without them, life feels a bit incomplete.

So enjoy the music.

Dance if you feel like dancing.

Sing if you feel like singing.

Don't worry too much about what people will think. Most beautiful memories begin the moment you stop caring about others' opinions.

See you in the next dance. ❤️🕺
